# mod-101-privacy-frameworks-and-position: Privacy Frameworks, Laws, and the Privacy-Engineer Position on the Governance Ladder

> Scaffolded by `aicg org execute-plan`. Lecture chapters and exercise content are authored on subsequent autonomous cycles.

**Estimated effort:** 12 hours

## Learning objectives

- Read GDPR Articles 5 (principles), 6 (lawful basis), 9 (special categories), 22 (automated decision-making), 25 (data protection by design and by default), 32 (security of processing), 35 (DPIA), and 17 (right to erasure) as an engineer — mapping each obligation to concrete AI-system controls, tests, and evidence artifacts
- Read EU AI Act Article 10 (data and data governance) and Article 15 (accuracy / robustness / cybersecurity) as the data-governance anchor; position Article 72 (post-market monitoring) as the privacy-monitoring interface
- Read CCPA / CPRA — including the CPPA Automated Decisionmaking Technology (ADMT) rules — and position the 20+ US state privacy-act matrix (VCDPA, CPA, CTDPA, UCPA, OCPA, TDPSA, TIPA, NJDPA, FDBR, NHIPA, MDPA, MCDPA, DPDPA, RICDPA, INCDPA) against the CCPA baseline
- Read sector-specific US laws — HIPAA Privacy / Security / Breach-Notification Rules, FERPA, COPPA, GLBA Safeguards Rule — and know which clauses trigger when the AI system touches PHI / student data / children / financial data
- Position international regimes — PIPL (PRC), PIPEDA (Canada), LGPD (Brazil), the OECD Privacy Guidelines — and the EDPB Opinion 28/2024 on AI models + CNIL AI sheets as the European regulator stance on AI + GDPR
- Read the NIST Privacy Framework (IDENTIFY-P / GOVERN-P / CONTROL-P / COMMUNICATE-P / PROTECT-P) and ISO/IEC 27701 (PIMS) / 29100 (privacy framework) as the engineering-management shapes an enterprise AI privacy program plugs into
- Diagram the role's exact scope on the level ladder — what this role owns vs. what `ai-governance-analyst` (level 15) already owns, what the peer `ai-risk-engineer` (level 25) already owns (mod-107 privacy-risk-engineering slice at working depth), and what `ai-evaluation-engineer` (level 35), `senior-ai-governance-architect` (level 50), and `head-of-ai-governance` (level 60) inherit or extend
