## Methodology

1. Sourced the canonical task domains for an AI Privacy Engineer (level 30, AI Governance family) from the public references catalogued in [`.aicg/job-requirements.json`](.aicg/job-requirements.json) `authoritative_references`.
2. Mapped each task domain to (a) the role on our level ladder that should own it primarily and (b) the curriculum module that will cover it.
3. Applied the **ownership rule** — assign coverage to the lowest-level role that genuinely requires the skill; higher-level tracks link back rather than duplicate. In particular, deferred:
   - *Down* to `ai-governance-analyst` (level 15) for operational privacy legwork this role consumes — ROPA upkeep, intake questionnaires, privacy-notice first-drafts, jurisdictional regulatory tracking, framework-crosswalk legwork.
   - *Down* to `ml-engineer` (level 20) for classical ML / PyTorch / sklearn / HF Transformers fundamentals; to `ai-infra-junior-engineer` (level 10) for engineering-craft prerequisites.
   - *Sideways* to `ai-risk-engineer` (peer, level 25, same family) for the broader harm-model / adversarial-ML / guardrail / MRM surface — its mod-107 privacy-risk-engineering slice is the foundation this packet deepens.
   - *Sideways* to `ai-infra-mlops` (peer, level 25, Infra family) for MLOps automation privacy gates plug into.
   - *Sideways* to `training-pipeline-engineer` (peer, level 25, ML Engineering family) for distributed-training integration.
   - *Sideways* to `ai-eval-engineer` (peer, level 30, AI Engineering family) for application-layer evaluation-engineering depth.
   - *Sideways* to `model-evaluation-engineer` (peer, level 30, ML Engineering family) for statistical calibration methodology depth on MIA / DP confidence intervals.
   - *Up* to `ai-evaluation-engineer` (peer / next-up, level 35, Governance family) for release-assurance / audit-trail / regulator-facing methodology depth.
   - *Up* to `security-learning` / `ai-infra-security` (level 35) for deep ML/AI security at platform scale (inference-edge model-extraction defence, enclave attestation at fleet scale, KMS / HSM integration depth).
   - *Up* to `agentic-safety-engineer` (level 40) for frontier-agent dangerous-capability evaluation depth beyond privacy.
   - *Up* to `senior-ai-governance-architect` (level 50) for control-library architecture, policy taxonomy, cross-jurisdiction reconciliation; to `head-of-ai-governance` (level 60) and `chief-ai-officer` (level 70) for program leadership and executive scope.
   - *Out of scope* to legal counsel / DPO for legal opinion and DPO statutory duties, and to security operations / DFIR for SOC-side breach handling (this role feeds AI-specific privacy signal to SecOps / Breach Response).
4. Flagged everything that has not yet been validated against in-window postings so the next cycle can demote any requirement whose evidence stays empty. All frequency cells below are marked `<!-- needs-research -->` until live posting evidence is collected.
